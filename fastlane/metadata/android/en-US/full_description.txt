YunX (云析) is an open-source share-link parser and high-speed downloader for Android: paste a share link and download it right away — cloud drives and video platforms alike.

Features
- Cloud-drive share links: Quark, UC, Xunlei, Baidu, 139, 123, 115, GuangYa, Lanzou and Lanzou Premium, with automatic passcode matching
- Video links: Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Kuaishou, Weibo and AcFun need no login; Bilibili resolves higher quality after signing in, Pixiv returns only part of the public works when signed out and the full set once signed in, and Bilibili's separate audio/video tracks are merged inside the app
- High-speed downloads: concurrent HTTP Range chunks with resume support, up to 32 parallel connections
- Online preview and playback: images, video and audio play right in the app (each request carries its own Referer/Cookie headers); common text files inside cloud drives can be opened too
- Two-column image grid: photo posts are laid out as a two-column grid — tap to view full screen with paging and pinch-zoom, long-press to multi-select and batch download
- Grouped downloads: a whole folder, or one batch of downloaded images, is collapsed into a single folder group on the Downloads page
- Temporary-save cleanup: Baidu and Xunlei transfers are cleaned up right after the direct link is fetched; Quark keeps them until the download finishes or the task is deleted
- Multi-provider login: WebView cookies for Quark/UC/Baidu/139/115, password or SMS for Xunlei, account+JWT for 123
- Credential backup: cookies and JWTs exported as AES-GCM encrypted files, keyed by a user passphrase
- Clipboard detection: copy a share link and the app offers one-tap parsing when you return to it

Supported providers
Cloud drives: Quark Drive, UC Drive, Xunlei Drive, Baidu Netdisk, 123 Cloud Drive, 139 Drive (HeCaiYun), 115 Drive, GuangYa Drive, Lanzou, Lanzou Premium
Video / galleries: Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Kuaishou, Weibo, AcFun, Bilibili, Pixiv

How to use
1. Sign in to the cloud drive accounts you need on the "Accounts" page (video platforms that require login are there too)
2. Paste a link (with or without passcode) on the "Parse" page; video links are recognised automatically
3. For cloud drives, browse the shared files and tap a file to resolve its direct link; for video links, preview or download straight from the result page — long-press images to multi-select
4. Track progress on the "Downloads" page; pause / resume / delete / open as you like

Requirements
Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer.

Ads and privacy
No ads and no analytics SDKs. Direct links, cookies and JWTs stay on your device; credential backups are encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase.

Note
Using Baidu Netdisk may trigger account risk control. YunX is free and open source forever — any paid copy is a repackaged scam and is unrelated to this project.
